What is inside
- Tier: T1 Wrap. The original Apple II program runs unchanged inside an
  emulator; it is not a recompiled or rewritten game.
- Emulator: a2emu from apple2tc (https://github.com/tmikov/apple2tc, MIT
  License, (c) Tzvetan Mikov), built with Apple2Forge patches that remove the
  built-in ROM and add a 16K Language Card. sokol (zlib/libpng License,
  (c) Andre Weissflog) provides the window, graphics and sound; nlohmann/json
  (MIT License, (c) Niels Lohmann) is linked in. Full license texts are in
  Contents/Resources/licenses/.
- Your disk image, copied unchanged.

What is NOT inside
- No Apple ROM. At launch the app reads your own Apple II+ ROM from
  ~/Library/Application Support/Apple2Forge/roms/apple2plus.rom
  and shows an alert if it is missing.

Logs: ~/Library/Logs/Apple2Forge/
